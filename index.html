About Midnight

Travel knowledge built one itinerary at a time.

Midnight Travel Consulting grew out of years spent researching routes, comparing fares, managing points, solving travel disruptions, and helping other people make better flight decisions.

The value is not access to a secret booking screen. It is having one person who understands the immediate trip and the wider picture—airline relationships, frequent-flyer earning, business programs, card benefits, and whether a points redemption is actually a good use of the balance.

The working style is direct and practical: narrow the choices, explain the tradeoffs, keep pricing visible, and make sure the traveler understands why a recommendation is being made.
