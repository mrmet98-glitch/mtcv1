How volume is counted

One flight booking means one ticketed passenger itinerary. A connecting or round-trip itinerary is not counted as a separate booking for every segment.

How tiers adjust

The working tier can be reviewed against a trailing three-month average so a temporary spike does not automatically reset the relationship.

What may require custom scope

Large groups, unusually complex changes, 24/7 coverage, or extensive non-air travel can be quoted separately before work begins.
